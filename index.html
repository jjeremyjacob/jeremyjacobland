Jeremy Jacob — Transmissions From Another World

Transmissions from another world. A living archive created by artist and filmmaker Jeremy Jacob. Moving images, performances, films, and visual fragments emerge from an imagined landscape between memory, movement, cinema, and transformation.
Upon awakening, they couldn’t seem to remember anything. Not as if life was blank, fully concealed or imperceptible, but as if everything in the world was a toy picked up and tossed into a bin with no specific relationship to any other object outside of its being jumbled together amongst other things. The mind had not, however, become a wasteland composed of miscellaneous things, but a kind of garden to be played with by the imagination. There was no reality here, only fantasy; at least nothing that could be pointed to and claimed to be any more or less real than any other thing and all was free to be placed here or there in any way most pleasing to the imaginative senses. It was not altogether clear where or when time was, if this thing came before or after that or when exactly it got thrown here and that there but things did seem to happen, first this and then that but the sequence could never be pinned down.
